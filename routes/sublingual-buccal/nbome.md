# NBOMe drugs (25I-NBOMe, 25C-NBOMe, 25B-NBOMe): Sublingual and buccal (under the tongue or in the cheek)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## About this route

Absorbed through the lining of the mouth, partly skipping first-pass metabolism. For NBOMe drugs (25I-NBOMe, 25C-NBOMe, 25B-NBOMe): Blotter held in the mouth.

## How this route feels different

Under the tongue or in the cheek: faster than swallowing.

## Timeline for this route

### Onset (15-60 min)

Effects begin 15 to 60 minutes after taking.

### Come-up (1 h)

Visuals and stimulation.

### Peak (2-4 h)

Peak at 2 to 4 hours.

### Plateau (4-6 h)

Plateau several hours.

### Comedown (6-10 h)

Taper over hours.

### After-effects (12-24 h)

Exhaustion.

### Next day

Next-day fatigue.

For the full, detailed description of each phase, see the [general NBOMe drugs (25I-NBOMe, 25C-NBOMe, 25B-NBOMe) page](../../drugs/nbome.md).

## Risks of this route

Faster, stronger effect.

## Overdose note

NBOMes cause seizures, overheating, heart attacks, kidney failure and death.

## Signs that someone needs help

- Seizures, very high temperature, or chest pain (especially with NBOMes or 2C drugs).
- Severe panic that does not calm with reassurance, or danger to self or others.
- Unresponsiveness.

## What to do

- Call 911 for physical symptoms. Good Samaritan laws in most US states protect callers.
- Move to a quiet, safe place and speak calmly.
- Stay with them; do not leave them alone.

Read more: [Psychedelic safety](../../safety/psychedelic-safety.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
