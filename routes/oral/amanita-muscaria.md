# Amanita muscaria (fly agaric): Oral (swallowed)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## About this route

Swallowed as tablets, capsules, or liquids. The drug passes through the gut and liver first (first-pass metabolism), which lowers and slows what reaches the brain. For Amanita muscaria (fly agaric): Eaten or in gummies.

## How this route feels different

Swallowed: slower onset and longer effects; harder to judge, which tempts people to take more.

## Timeline for this route

### Onset (30 min-2 h)

Effects begin 30 minutes to 2 hours after eating.

### Come-up (1-2 h)

Nausea and drowsiness.

### Peak (2-3 h)

Peak at 2 to 3 hours.

### Plateau (3-6 h)

Several hours.

### Comedown (6-10 h)

Slow fade.

### After-effects (hours)

Fatigue.

### Next day

Next-day fog.

For the full, detailed description of each phase, see the [general Amanita muscaria (fly agaric) page](../../drugs/amanita-muscaria.md).

## Risks of this route

Delayed effects tempt redosing; edibles and long-acting pills are a common cause of accidental overdose and emergency visits.

## Overdose note

Poisoning causes confusion, seizures and coma.

## Signs that someone needs help

- Seizures, unresponsiveness, chest pain, or severe confusion.

## What to do

- Call 911 or Poison Control (1-800-222-1222).
- Recovery position if drowsy.

Read more: [Other psychoactives safety](../../safety/other-psychoactives-safety.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
