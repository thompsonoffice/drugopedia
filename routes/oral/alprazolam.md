# Alprazolam (Xanax): Oral (swallowed)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Never stop a daily benzodiazepine suddenly: withdrawal can cause seizures. Counterfeit pills often contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines.

## About this route

Swallowed as tablets, capsules, or liquids. The drug passes through the gut and liver first (first-pass metabolism), which lowers and slows what reaches the brain. For Alprazolam (Xanax): Immediate-release and extended-release tablets, orally disintegrating tablets, oral concentrate.

## How this route feels different

Swallowing is the standard medical route. Effects build gradually, with no rush; food can slow onset.

## Timeline for this route

### Onset (15-30 min)

Alprazolam usually begins within 15 to 30 minutes of swallowing: a softening of tension, shoulders dropping, and breathing that feels easier.

### Come-up (50-90 min)

Within the next 15 to 30 minutes, calm deepens and worries lose their grip. People feel loose, talkative, and unconcerned.

### Peak (1-2 h)

The peak, around 1 to 2 hours, brings strong relaxation, mild euphoria, and disinhibition. Speech can slur, balance worsens, and memory begins to drop out.

### Plateau (2-4 h)

The plateau is a few hours of calm, sleepy, and sometimes foggy state.

### Comedown (6-8 h total)

Effects last about 6 to 8 hours in total for immediate-release (PsychonautWiki). Rebound anxiety, irritability, and low mood can arrive sharply.

### After-effects (8-24 h)

After-effects include grogginess, memory gaps, and sometimes a 'benzo hangover'.

### Next day

After regular use, interdose withdrawal is common: anxiety between doses. Stopping suddenly after daily use can cause seizures.

For the full, detailed description of each phase, see the [general Alprazolam (Xanax) page](../../drugs/alprazolam.md).

## Risks of this route

Slow onset tempts people to take more before the first amount peaks. Long-acting drugs and their metabolites build up over days, especially in older adults, causing falls, confusion, and impaired driving. Counterfeit pills may contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines.

## Overdose note

Alone, alprazolam overdose causes deep sedation and confusion. With opioids or alcohol, it is a leading contributor to overdose deaths. Withdrawal seizures are a particular risk because it is short-acting and potent. Counterfeit 'bars' may contain fentanyl.

## Signs of a benzodiazepine overdose or dangerous sedation

- Extreme drowsiness, cannot be woken, or slurred, confused speech.
- Stumbling, poor coordination, or collapse.
- Slow or shallow breathing, especially if opioids or alcohol are involved.
- Blue or gray lips and fingertips.

## What to do

- Call 911. Good Samaritan laws in most US states protect people who call for help.
- If opioids may be involved (including counterfeit pills), give naloxone; it will not reverse the benzo but can reverse the opioid part.
- Put them on their side in the recovery position and stay with them.

Read more: [Benzodiazepine safety: overdose, mixing, blackouts, and withdrawal](../../safety/benzodiazepine-safety.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
