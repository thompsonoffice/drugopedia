# Datura and brugmansia (jimsonweed, angel's trumpet): Oral (swallowed)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## About this route

Swallowed as tablets, capsules, or liquids. The drug passes through the gut and liver first (first-pass metabolism), which lowers and slows what reaches the brain. For Datura and brugmansia (jimsonweed, angel's trumpet): Plant parts eaten or brewed.

## How this route feels different

Swallowed: slower onset and longer effects; harder to judge, which tempts people to take more.

## Timeline for this route

### Onset (30-60 min)

Effects begin within 30 minutes to 1 hour.

### Come-up (1-3 h)

Dry mouth, wide pupils, confusion.

### Peak (3-12 h)

Delirium peaks over several hours.

### Plateau (12-48 h)

Delirium can last 24 to 48 hours.

### Comedown (1-3 days)

Slow recovery over days.

### After-effects (days)

Blurred vision for days.

### Next day (days)

Memory gaps.

For the full, detailed description of each phase, see the [general Datura and brugmansia (jimsonweed, angel's trumpet) page](../../drugs/datura.md).

## Risks of this route

Delayed effects tempt redosing; edibles and long-acting pills are a common cause of accidental overdose and emergency visits.

## Overdose note

Datura poisoning causes delirium, overheating, seizures, coma and death.

## Signs that someone needs help

- Confusion, talking to people who are not there.
- Hot, dry, flushed skin; very fast heartbeat.
- Seizures or collapse.

## What to do

- Call 911 or Poison Control (1-800-222-1222).
- Keep them safe and calm; they may not know where they are.

Read more: [Deliriant safety](../../safety/deliriant-safety.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
