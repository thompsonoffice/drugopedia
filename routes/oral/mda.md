# MDA (sass, sally): Oral (swallowed)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## About this route

Swallowed as tablets, capsules, or liquids. The drug passes through the gut and liver first (first-pass metabolism), which lowers and slows what reaches the brain. For MDA (sass, sally): Capsules or crystals.

## How this route feels different

Swallowed: slower onset and longer effects; harder to judge, which tempts people to take more.

## Timeline for this route

### Onset (45-90 min)

Effects begin 45 to 90 minutes after swallowing.

### Come-up (1-2 h)

Energy and euphoria rise.

### Peak (2-3 h)

Peak 2 to 3 hours.

### Plateau (3-5 h)

Plateau 2 to 3 hours.

### Comedown (5-8 h)

Long comedown.

### After-effects (8-24 h)

Insomnia.

### Next day (2-4 days)

Low mood for days.

For the full, detailed description of each phase, see the [general MDA (sass, sally) page](../../drugs/mda.md).

## Risks of this route

Delayed effects tempt redosing; edibles and long-acting pills are a common cause of accidental overdose and emergency visits.

## Overdose note

Overheating, seizures and serotonin toxicity are the main risks.

## Signs that someone needs help

- Very high temperature, confusion, or stopping sweating.
- Severe headache, vomiting and confusion after drinking lots of water (low sodium).
- Muscle rigidity, jerking, or seizures.
- Collapse.

## What to do

- Call 911 and tell responders what was taken.
- Move to a cool place; cool the skin.
- Do not give large amounts of water.

Read more: [Empathogen (MDMA) safety](../../safety/empathogen-safety.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
