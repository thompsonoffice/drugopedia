# Salvia divinorum: Inhaled and smoked

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## About this route

Vapor or smoke absorbed through the lungs, reaching the brain within seconds. For Salvia divinorum: Smoked.

## How this route feels different

Smoked or vaped: effects in seconds to minutes, shorter duration, and strong reinforcement; damages the lungs and airways.

## Timeline for this route

### Onset (seconds)

Effects begin within seconds.

### Come-up (under 1 min)

Rapid loss of contact.

### Peak (1-5 min)

Peak within 1 to 5 minutes.

### Plateau (5-10 min)

Short plateau.

### Comedown (15-30 min)

Return over 15 to 30 minutes.

### After-effects (hours)

Afterglow or unease.

### Next day (none)

Usually none.

For the full, detailed description of each phase, see the [general Salvia divinorum page](../../drugs/salvia.md).

## Risks of this route

Lung and airway damage, burns, and very fast, compulsive use patterns.

## Overdose note

Accidents are the main danger.

## Signs that someone needs help

- Seizures, unresponsiveness, chest pain, or severe confusion.

## What to do

- Call 911 or Poison Control (1-800-222-1222).
- Recovery position if drowsy.

Read more: [Other psychoactives safety](../../safety/other-psychoactives-safety.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
