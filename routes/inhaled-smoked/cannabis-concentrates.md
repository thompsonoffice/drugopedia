# Cannabis concentrates (wax, shatter, dabs, vape oil): Inhaled and smoked

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## About this route

Vapor or smoke absorbed through the lungs, reaching the brain within seconds. For Cannabis concentrates (wax, shatter, dabs, vape oil): Vaporized ('dabbed') or vaped from cartridges.

## How this route feels different

Smoked or vaped: effects in seconds to minutes, shorter duration, and strong reinforcement; damages the lungs and airways.

## Timeline for this route

### Onset (seconds)

Inhaled concentrates take effect within seconds: a strong head rush and pounding heart.

### Come-up (2-10 min)

Within minutes the high rises steeply; coughing is common.

### Peak (10-30 min)

The peak is intense and early: disorientation, heavy body, and for some, panic.

### Plateau (30-60 min)

The plateau lasts about an hour.

### Comedown (1-3 h)

Fades over 1 to 3 hours.

### After-effects (2-4 h)

Heavy fatigue and hunger.

### Next day (next morning)

Daily users can feel foggy and irritable between uses.

For the full, detailed description of each phase, see the [general Cannabis concentrates (wax, shatter, dabs, vape oil) page](../../drugs/cannabis-concentrates.md).

## Risks of this route

Lung and airway damage, burns, and very fast, compulsive use patterns.

## Overdose note

Concentrates cause more panic, fainting, vomiting and psychosis emergencies than flower; illicit vape carts can contain harmful additives.

## Signs that someone needs help

- Panic, chest pain, fainting, or a very fast heartbeat.
- Severe confusion, paranoia, or hallucinations.
- Repeated vomiting.
- A child who may have eaten an edible.
- With synthetic cannabinoids: seizures, unresponsiveness, or violent agitation.

## What to do

- Stay calm, move to a quiet place, and reassure them.
- Call 911 for chest pain, seizures, unresponsiveness, or a child. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.
- Use the recovery position if they are drowsy and vomiting.

Read more: [Cannabis safety: edibles, high-potency products, synthetic cannabinoids](../../safety/cannabis-safety.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
