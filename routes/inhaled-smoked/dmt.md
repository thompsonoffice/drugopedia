# DMT (N,N-dimethyltryptamine): Inhaled and smoked

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## About this route

Vapor or smoke absorbed through the lungs, reaching the brain within seconds. For DMT (N,N-dimethyltryptamine): Vaporized or smoked.

## How this route feels different

Smoked or vaped: effects in seconds to minutes, shorter duration, and strong reinforcement; damages the lungs and airways.

## Timeline for this route

### Onset (seconds)

Effects begin within seconds of inhaling.

### Come-up (30 s-1 min)

A rapid rush of sound and visuals.

### Peak (2-5 min)

Peak at 2 to 5 minutes, with loss of contact with surroundings.

### Plateau (5-10 min)

Plateau a few minutes.

### Comedown (10-20 min)

Return over 10 to 20 minutes.

### After-effects (under 1 h)

Afterglow.

### Next day (none)

Usually none.

For the full, detailed description of each phase, see the [general DMT (N,N-dimethyltryptamine) page](../../drugs/dmt.md).

## Risks of this route

Lung and airway damage, burns, and very fast, compulsive use patterns.

## Overdose note

Physical toxicity is low, but blood pressure spikes and falls are risks, especially with heart disease.

## Signs that someone needs help

- Seizures, very high temperature, or chest pain (especially with NBOMes or 2C drugs).
- Severe panic that does not calm with reassurance, or danger to self or others.
- Unresponsiveness.

## What to do

- Call 911 for physical symptoms. Good Samaritan laws in most US states protect callers.
- Move to a quiet, safe place and speak calmly.
- Stay with them; do not leave them alone.

Read more: [Psychedelic safety](../../safety/psychedelic-safety.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
