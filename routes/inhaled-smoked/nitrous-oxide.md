# Nitrous oxide (laughing gas): Inhaled and smoked

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## About this route

Vapor or smoke absorbed through the lungs, reaching the brain within seconds. For Nitrous oxide (laughing gas): Inhaled gas.

## How this route feels different

Smoked or vaped: effects in seconds to minutes, shorter duration, and strong reinforcement; damages the lungs and airways.

## Timeline for this route

### Onset (seconds)

Effects start within seconds of inhaling.

### Come-up (10-20 s)

A rush of tingling and sound distortion follows.

### Peak (20-60 s)

The peak lasts seconds to a minute.

### Plateau (1-2 min)

Brief plateau.

### Comedown (2-5 min)

Effects fade within 2 to 5 minutes.

### After-effects (minutes)

Brief headache or dizziness.

### Next day (none (repeated use: nerve damage))

Normally none, but repeated heavy use causes nerve damage.

For the full, detailed description of each phase, see the [general Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) page](../../drugs/nitrous-oxide.md).

## Risks of this route

Lung and airway damage, burns, and very fast, compulsive use patterns.

## Overdose note

Deaths come from suffocation (especially with bags or masks), falls and accidents; heavy use causes B12-related nerve damage.

## Signs that someone needs help

- Unresponsive, or breathing slowly or noisily.
- Vomiting while dazed.
- Very high temperature, seizures, or extreme agitation (especially PCP or DXM).
- Injury they cannot feel.

## What to do

- Call 911. Good Samaritan laws in most US states protect callers.
- Put them in the recovery position and keep them away from water, stairs and roads.
- Give naloxone if opioids may be involved.

Read more: [Dissociative safety: K-holes, injuries, bladder damage, mixing](../../safety/dissociative-safety.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
