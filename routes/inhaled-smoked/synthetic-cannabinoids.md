# Synthetic cannabinoids (K2, Spice): Inhaled and smoked

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## About this route

Vapor or smoke absorbed through the lungs, reaching the brain within seconds. For Synthetic cannabinoids (K2, Spice): Smoked or vaped; also sprayed on paper in prisons.

## How this route feels different

Smoked or vaped: effects in seconds to minutes, shorter duration, and strong reinforcement; damages the lungs and airways.

## Timeline for this route

### Onset (seconds-minutes)

Effects begin within seconds to minutes of smoking.

### Come-up (2-10 min)

The rise is fast and heavy, with a racing heart.

### Peak (10-30 min)

The peak can include confusion, paranoia, vomiting, or unresponsiveness.

### Plateau (30-90 min)

Effects are relatively short, often 1 to 2 hours.

### Comedown (1-3 h)

Comedown brings anxiety and craving.

### After-effects (3-6 h)

Heavy fatigue; for some, lingering psychosis.

### Next day

Daily users experience withdrawal: sweating, vomiting, insomnia, irritability.

For the full, detailed description of each phase, see the [general Synthetic cannabinoids (K2, Spice) page](../../drugs/synthetic-cannabinoids.md).

## Risks of this route

Lung and airway damage, burns, and very fast, compulsive use patterns.

## Overdose note

Synthetic cannabinoids cause seizures, kidney injury, heart attacks, strokes, psychosis and deaths; there is no antidote.

## Signs that someone needs help

- Panic, chest pain, fainting, or a very fast heartbeat.
- Severe confusion, paranoia, or hallucinations.
- Repeated vomiting.
- A child who may have eaten an edible.
- With synthetic cannabinoids: seizures, unresponsiveness, or violent agitation.

## What to do

- Stay calm, move to a quiet place, and reassure them.
- Call 911 for chest pain, seizures, unresponsiveness, or a child. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.
- Use the recovery position if they are drowsy and vomiting.

Read more: [Cannabis safety: edibles, high-potency products, synthetic cannabinoids](../../safety/cannabis-safety.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
