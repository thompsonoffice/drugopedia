# RoxyBond and Oxaydo

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Counterfeit pills made to look like these brands often contain fentanyl or other drugs.

## What it contains

The opioid or main ingredient is [Oxycodone](../drugs/oxycodone.md).

## History

Abuse-deterrent immediate-release oxycodone tablets (Oxaydo 2011, RoxyBond 2017).

## How the combination changes the feel

Same as plain immediate-release oxycodone when swallowed as directed.

## What it feels like to the touch

Tablets designed to resist misuse. Touch and shape cannot confirm what a pill is: ask a pharmacist, use a pill-imprint app or a fentanyl test strip.

## Overdose and safety

Opioid overdose signs: cannot be woken, slow or stopped breathing, blue lips, pinpoint pupils. Call 911 and give [naloxone](../narcan/naloxone.md). Read [opioid overdose](../safety/opioid-overdose.md). 

For the full drug page, see [Oxycodone](../drugs/oxycodone.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
