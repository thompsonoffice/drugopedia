# Lortab

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Counterfeit pills made to look like these brands often contain fentanyl or other drugs.

## What it contains

The opioid or main ingredient is [Hydrocodone](../drugs/hydrocodone.md), combined with: acetaminophen.

## History

Brand by UCB, also sold as an elixir (liquid) with a fruity flavor, which raised risks of measuring errors.

## How the combination changes the feel

Similar to Vicodin and Norco; the liquid works a bit faster.

## What it feels like over time

Times vary between people; this describes swallowed tablets.

### Onset

Lortab, a hydrocodone-acetaminophen brand, begins with hydrocodone's soft, rounded warmth: a loosening in the chest and stomach, a gentle heaviness creeping into the arms, and shoulders that drop without effort. The syrup form, sweet and fruit-flavored, is absorbed somewhat faster than tablets, so its first effects can arrive a little sooner.

Mentally the onset is mellow and easygoing. Tension and irritability ease, and people describe feeling cozy and settled rather than energized; thinking slows slightly but stays clear.

An early tickling itch on the face, neck or upper back is common, and some feel queasy, especially on an empty stomach. The mouth dries and pupils narrow. Because every tablet or measure also contains acetaminophen, the liver risk travels with each use.

### Peak

At the peak the body feels warm, heavy and comforted, with waves of tingling and itch, especially on the face and upper body. Breathing slows and pupils narrow.

The emotional tone is calm, sleepy contentment, the same soft, dreamy hydrocodone character as Norco and Vicodin rather than Percocet's brighter lift.

Breathing is most suppressed now. Because Lortab syrup is a liquid, measuring errors can lead to taking far more than intended, which is especially dangerous for children. Because each tablet also contains acetaminophen, taking more to chase or extend the effect quietly raises the risk of liver injury, which may cause no symptoms for a day or more. Alcohol, fasting, liver disease, and other acetaminophen-containing products (many cold and sleep medicines) make it worse.

### Plateau

Lortab's plateau is a long, warm heaviness. The body sinks into the bed or couch, eyelids feel weighted, and people may nod off and startle awake. Itch drifts from place to place, and a dry, sticky mouth persists; with the syrup, a lingering sweet taste is common.

Mentally the plateau is passive and dreamy. Attention slides off tasks, half-dreams appear when the eyes close, and the mood is contented but sleepy. People caring for someone on Lortab syrup often notice slurred, slow speech in this phase.

Constipation and slow urination build, the face may be flushed, and breathing stays slowed. Sleeping through this phase after alcohol or another sedative, or after a mismeasured dose of syrup, is a common overdose setting, especially for children.

### Comedown

The comedown is gradual: warmth leaves the limbs first, then tiredness, a dull headache and returning aches take its place.

Emotionally people feel flat, sluggish or slightly low; regular users notice restlessness, yawning and a pull toward the next dose.

Nausea and constipation can linger into the next day. Repeat doses to avoid the comedown build tolerance and add acetaminophen to the liver each time.

## What the added ingredients bring, and their risks

**Acetaminophen (paracetamol):** adds pain and fever relief without extra sedation, but it is the most common cause of acute liver failure in the US. Liver damage can happen silently over a day or two, with few symptoms until it is severe, and is more likely with alcohol, fasting, liver disease, or taking more than one acetaminophen product at once (many cold medicines contain it). In 2011 the FDA limited the acetaminophen in prescription combinations and added a boxed warning about liver injury. Overdose needs hospital treatment even if the person feels fine.

## What it feels like to the touch

An older DailyMed Lortab tablet label (UCB) describes white tablets with pink specks, capsule-shaped and bisected, debossed 'ucb/901'; other Lortab strengths and later makers used different imprints. DailyMed also lists Lortab as a syrup (formerly called Lortab Elixir). Liquids carry a high risk of measuring mistakes and are dangerous for children. **Touch, shape and imprint cannot confirm what a pill is.** Counterfeit copies of these brands are pressed to look and feel real and often contain fentanyl, xylazine or designer benzodiazepines. Ask a pharmacist, read the imprint with Be My Eyes or Seeing AI and look it up on DailyMed, keep medicine in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## Overdose and safety

Opioid overdose signs: cannot be woken, slow or stopped breathing, blue lips, pinpoint pupils. Call 911 and give [naloxone](../narcan/naloxone.md). Read [opioid overdose](../safety/opioid-overdose.md). If too much was taken, the acetaminophen can destroy the liver even when the person seems fine; go to an emergency department or call Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) right away, since an antidote (acetylcysteine) works best early. 

For the full drug page, see [Hydrocodone](../drugs/hydrocodone.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
