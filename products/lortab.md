# Lortab

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Counterfeit pills made to look like these brands often contain fentanyl or other drugs.

## What it contains

The opioid or main ingredient is [Hydrocodone](../drugs/hydrocodone.md), combined with: acetaminophen.

## History

Brand by UCB, also sold as an elixir (liquid) with a fruity flavor, which raised risks of measuring errors.

## How the combination changes the feel

Similar to Vicodin and Norco; the liquid works a bit faster.

## What it feels like over time

Times vary between people; this describes swallowed tablets.

### Onset

Lortab, another hydrocodone-acetaminophen brand, has the same mellow onset: warmth in the chest and stomach and loosening in the limbs. The liquid form starts a little faster than tablets.

Mentally the onset is calm and relaxed.

Itch and nausea can appear early.

### Peak

The peak brings warm, heavy comfort and tingling.

The emotional tone is calm, sleepy contentment.

Breathing is most suppressed. Because each tablet also contains acetaminophen, taking more to chase or extend the effect quietly raises the risk of liver injury, which may cause no symptoms for a day or more. Alcohol, fasting, liver disease, and other acetaminophen-containing products (many cold and sleep medicines) make it worse.

### Plateau

The plateau is drowsy and heavy.

Mentally people drift.

Breathing remains slowed and constipation builds.

### Comedown

The comedown is gradual.

Mood is flat or sleepy.

Repeat use adds dependence and liver risk.

## What the added ingredients bring, and their risks

**Acetaminophen (paracetamol):** adds pain and fever relief without extra sedation, but it is the most common cause of acute liver failure in the US. Liver damage can happen silently over a day or two, with few symptoms until it is severe, and is more likely with alcohol, fasting, liver disease, or taking more than one acetaminophen product at once (many cold medicines contain it). In 2011 the FDA limited the acetaminophen in prescription combinations and added a boxed warning about liver injury. Overdose needs hospital treatment even if the person feels fine.

## What it feels like to the touch

Lortab tablets are capsule-shaped or oval, scored, and debossed with the brand name, in white or lightly tinted colors depending on strength. Lortab was also sold as a liquid (Lortab Elixir), a sweet, fruit-flavored syrup; liquids carry a high risk of measuring mistakes and are dangerous for children. **Touch, shape and imprint cannot confirm what a pill is.** Counterfeit copies of these brands are pressed to look and feel real and often contain fentanyl, xylazine or designer benzodiazepines. Ask a pharmacist, read the imprint with Be My Eyes or Seeing AI and look it up on DailyMed, keep medicine in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## Overdose and safety

Opioid overdose signs: cannot be woken, slow or stopped breathing, blue lips, pinpoint pupils. Call 911 and give [naloxone](../narcan/naloxone.md). Read [opioid overdose](../safety/opioid-overdose.md). If too much was taken, the acetaminophen can destroy the liver even when the person seems fine; go to an emergency department or call Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) right away, since an antidote (acetylcysteine) works best early. 

For the full drug page, see [Hydrocodone](../drugs/hydrocodone.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
