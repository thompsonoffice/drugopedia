# Percodan

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Counterfeit pills made to look like these brands often contain fentanyl or other drugs.

## What it contains

The opioid or main ingredient is [Oxycodone](../drugs/oxycodone.md), combined with: aspirin.

## History

Introduced in 1950 by Endo; it was one of the most misused drugs of the 1960s.

## How the combination changes the feel

The aspirin adds anti-inflammatory relief; stomach upset and bleeding risk are higher than with Percocet.

## What the added ingredients bring, and their risks

**Aspirin:** adds anti-inflammatory pain relief but thins the blood and irritates the stomach, raising the risk of stomach bleeding and ulcers, especially with alcohol. Large amounts cause aspirin poisoning: ringing in the ears, fast breathing, confusion, and acid-base problems. Not for children or teens with viral illness (Reye's syndrome).

## What it feels like to the touch

Yellow, round, scored tablets historically. Touch and shape cannot confirm what a pill is: ask a pharmacist, use a pill-imprint app or a fentanyl test strip.

## Overdose and safety

Opioid overdose signs: cannot be woken, slow or stopped breathing, blue lips, pinpoint pupils. Call 911 and give [naloxone](../narcan/naloxone.md). Read [opioid overdose](../safety/opioid-overdose.md). 

For the full drug page, see [Oxycodone](../drugs/oxycodone.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
