# Vicoprofen

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Counterfeit pills made to look like these brands often contain fentanyl or other drugs.

## What it contains

The opioid or main ingredient is [Hydrocodone](../drugs/hydrocodone.md), combined with: ibuprofen.

## History

Approved in 1997, combining hydrocodone with ibuprofen instead of acetaminophen.

## How the combination changes the feel

Stronger relief for inflammatory pain (dental, injuries); the opioid feel is similar. Stomach upset is more common.

## What the added ingredients bring, and their risks

**Ibuprofen:** adds anti-inflammatory relief; it can cause stomach bleeding, kidney injury (worse with dehydration), and raises heart and blood-pressure risks.

## What it feels like to the touch

Film-coated tablets. Touch and shape cannot confirm what a pill is: ask a pharmacist, use a pill-imprint app or a fentanyl test strip.
