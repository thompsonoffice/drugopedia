# Tuzistra XR and other codeine cough products

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Counterfeit pills made to look like these brands often contain fentanyl or other drugs.

## What it contains

The opioid or main ingredient is [Codeine](../drugs/codeine.md), combined with: chlorpheniramine.

## History

Extended-release codeine-chlorpheniramine cough suspension; other codeine-promethazine syrups are the basis of 'lean'.

## How the combination changes the feel

Sleepy, heavy, long-lasting calm.

## What the added ingredients bring, and their risks

**Chlorpheniramine:** a sedating antihistamine that adds drowsiness, dry mouth, and urinary retention, and deepens breathing depression.

## What it feels like to the touch

Liquids. See the Lean page for promethazine-codeine. Touch and shape cannot confirm what a pill is: ask a pharmacist, use a pill-imprint app or a fentanyl test strip.

## Overdose and safety



For the full drug page, see [Codeine](../drugs/codeine.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
