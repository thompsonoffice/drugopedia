# Tussionex

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Counterfeit pills made to look like these brands often contain fentanyl or other drugs.

## What it contains

The opioid or main ingredient is [Hydrocodone](../drugs/hydrocodone.md), combined with: chlorpheniramine.

## History

An extended-release cough suspension (hydrocodone and chlorpheniramine on a slow-release resin), given every 12 hours.

## How the combination changes the feel

Heavy, long sleepiness and quieted cough; the antihistamine adds dry mouth and grogginess.

## What the added ingredients bring, and their risks

**Chlorpheniramine:** a sedating antihistamine that adds drowsiness, dry mouth, and urinary retention, and deepens breathing depression.

## What it feels like to the touch

A liquid suspension. Misuse in place of cough syrups has caused fatal breathing depression, including in children. Touch and shape cannot confirm what a pill is: ask a pharmacist, use a pill-imprint app or a fentanyl test strip.
