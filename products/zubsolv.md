# Zubsolv

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Counterfeit pills made to look like these brands often contain fentanyl or other drugs.

## What it contains

The opioid or main ingredient is [Buprenorphine (Subutex, Suboxone)](../drugs/buprenorphine.md), combined with: naloxone.

## History

A buprenorphine-naloxone sublingual tablet approved in 2013 that dissolves faster and absorbs more efficiently, so its strengths are not interchangeable with Suboxone.

## How the combination changes the feel

Similar to Suboxone; some prefer the taste and faster dissolving.

## What the added ingredients bring, and their risks

**Naloxone (in the tablet or film):** poorly absorbed when the product dissolves under the tongue as directed, so it does little. It is included to discourage injection, which can trigger sudden withdrawal.

## What it feels like to the touch

Small tablets with a menthol flavor. Touch and shape cannot confirm what a pill is: ask a pharmacist, use a pill-imprint app or a fentanyl test strip.

## Overdose and safety

Opioid overdose signs: cannot be woken, slow or stopped breathing, blue lips, pinpoint pupils. Call 911 and give [naloxone](../narcan/naloxone.md). Read [opioid overdose](../safety/opioid-overdose.md). 

For the full drug page, see [Buprenorphine (Subutex, Suboxone)](../drugs/buprenorphine.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
