# Combunox

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Counterfeit pills made to look like these brands often contain fentanyl or other drugs.

## What it contains

The opioid or main ingredient is [Oxycodone](../drugs/oxycodone.md), combined with: ibuprofen.

## History

Approved in 2004 and later discontinued.

## How the combination changes the feel

Ibuprofen added anti-inflammatory relief.

## What the added ingredients bring, and their risks

**Ibuprofen:** adds anti-inflammatory relief; it can cause stomach bleeding, kidney injury (worse with dehydration), and raises heart and blood-pressure risks.

## What it feels like to the touch

Tablets. Touch and shape cannot confirm what a pill is: ask a pharmacist, use a pill-imprint app or a fentanyl test strip.

## Overdose and safety



For the full drug page, see [Oxycodone](../drugs/oxycodone.md).

## Getting help

SAMHSA National Helpline: **1-800-662-4357** (free, confidential, 24/7, English and Spanish). In an emergency, call **911**.
