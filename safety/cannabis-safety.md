# Cannabis safety

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice. No amounts, preparation, or sourcing. In an emergency in the US, call 911. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222. SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357.

## Why this page exists

Cannabis is widely legal and often seen as harmless, but today's products are much stronger than in the past, and synthetic and hemp-derived cannabinoids add new dangers.

## The biggest dangers

### Edibles

Edibles take 30 minutes to 2 hours to begin, so people take more and become overwhelmed. They are the leading cause of cannabis poisonings in children. Store them locked away.

### High-potency products

Concentrates and strong flower raise the risk of panic, psychosis, and cannabis use disorder. Young people and people with family history of psychosis are most at risk.

### Synthetic cannabinoids (K2, Spice)

These are far stronger than THC and cause seizures, kidney injury, heart attacks, psychosis and death.

### Hemp-derived products

Delta-8, HHC and THC-O are made in unregulated labs and labels are often wrong.

### Driving

Cannabis slows reaction time and impairs judgment. Do not drive after using.

### Cannabinoid hyperemesis syndrome

Heavy long-term use can cause repeated severe vomiting. The cure is stopping cannabis.

### Mixing

Cannabis with alcohol increases nausea, dizziness and impairment. Cannabis can change levels of some medicines, including blood thinners.

## Warning signs

- Chest pain, fainting, or very fast heartbeat.
- Severe paranoia, hallucinations or confusion.
- Repeated vomiting.
- A child who may have eaten an edible.

## What to do

1. Stay calm and move to a quiet place.
2. Call 911 for chest pain, seizures, unresponsiveness, or a child exposure. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.
3. Recovery position if drowsy and vomiting.
