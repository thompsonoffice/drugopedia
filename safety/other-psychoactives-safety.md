# Other psychoactives safety

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice. No amounts, preparation, or sourcing. In an emergency in the US, call 911. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222. SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357.

## Key risks by group
- **Sedating medicines (promethazine, quetiapine, trazodone):** deeper sedation and stopped breathing when mixed with opioids, alcohol or benzodiazepines.
- **Bupropion:** seizures at high levels.
- **Tricyclic antidepressants:** seizures and fatal heart rhythms in overdose.
- **Kava:** liver injury.
- **Nutmeg and amanita:** long, unpleasant poisoning; the deadly death cap mushroom can be mistaken for amanita.
- **Salvia:** accidents during short dissociation.
- **Betel:** oral cancer with long-term use.
- **Kanna:** serotonin syndrome with antidepressants.

## What to do
Call 911 or Poison Control for seizures, chest pain, confusion, or unresponsiveness.
