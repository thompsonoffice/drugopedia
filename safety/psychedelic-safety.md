# Psychedelic safety

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice. No amounts, preparation, or sourcing. In an emergency in the US, call 911. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222. SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357. Fireside Project psychedelic peer support line: call or text 62-FIRESIDE (623-473-7433).

## Why this page exists

Classic psychedelics (LSD, psilocybin, DMT, mescaline) have low physical toxicity, but they can cause frightening experiences, risky behavior, and in vulnerable people lasting mental health problems. Some drugs sold as psychedelics (NBOMes, DOx drugs) are physically dangerous. Ibogaine and 5-MeO-DMT carry special risks.

## The biggest dangers

### Fake or mislabeled drugs

Blotters sold as LSD can contain NBOMes or DOx drugs, which have caused seizures and deaths. A bitter or numbing taste is a warning sign.

### Mental health

People with a personal or family history of psychosis, schizophrenia, or bipolar disorder are at higher risk of lasting psychosis or mania.

### Accidents

Falls, traffic, water, heights, and wandering off cause many psychedelic deaths.

### Interactions

- Lithium with psychedelics has been linked to seizures.
- MAO inhibitors (including ayahuasca) with SSRIs, MDMA, tramadol, DXM, 5-MeO-DMT or stimulants can cause serotonin syndrome or dangerous blood pressure.
- Ibogaine causes dangerous heart rhythms, especially with other heart-affecting drugs or after opioids.

### Lasting perception changes

A small number of people develop hallucinogen persisting perception disorder (HPPD).

## Helping someone in distress

1. Move to a calm, quiet, safe place.
2. Speak gently; remind them the drug will wear off.
3. Stay with them.
4. Call 911 for seizures, chest pain, very high temperature, unresponsiveness, or danger to self or others.
