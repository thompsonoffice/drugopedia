# Empathogen (MDMA) safety

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice. No amounts, preparation, or sourcing. In an emergency in the US, call 911. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222. SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357. Fireside Project psychedelic peer support line: call or text 62-FIRESIDE (623-473-7433).

## Why this page exists

MDMA and its relatives (MDA, benzofurans) cause most of their deaths through overheating, low blood sodium, and drug interactions. Pills and 'molly' are often something else: cathinones, PMA/PMMA, methamphetamine, or fentanyl.

## The biggest dangers

### Overheating

MDMA impairs temperature control. Hot venues, dancing, and dehydration can push body temperature to dangerous levels, causing organ failure.

### Low blood sodium (hyponatremia)

MDMA makes the body hold water. Drinking very large amounts of water can dilute blood sodium and swell the brain. Headache, vomiting and confusion after heavy water drinking are emergency signs, especially in women.

### Serotonin syndrome

Combining with antidepressants (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs), tramadol, DXM, lithium, or other serotonergic drugs can cause fever, rigidity, jerking and death.

### Mislabeled pills

PMA and PMMA feel weak, so people take more and overheat. Cathinones like eutylone keep people awake and agitated.

### Heart and brain

MDMA raises heart rate and blood pressure; 4-FA has been linked to strokes.

## Warning signs

- Very hot, confused, or stopped sweating.
- Severe headache, vomiting, confusion.
- Muscle stiffness, jerking, seizures.
- Collapse.

## What to do

1. Call 911 and tell responders what was taken.
2. Move to a cool place and cool the skin.
3. Do not give large amounts of water.
4. Recovery position if drowsy.
