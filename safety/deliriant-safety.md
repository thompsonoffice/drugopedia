# Deliriant safety

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice. No amounts, preparation, or sourcing. In an emergency in the US, call 911. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222. SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357.

## The biggest dangers
Deliriants (diphenhydramine, dimenhydrinate, datura, scopolamine) cause true delirium: people cannot tell what is real. Overheating, seizures and heart rhythm problems can be fatal.

## Warning signs
- Hot, dry, red skin; wide pupils.
- Very fast heartbeat.
- Confusion, talking to people who are not there.
- Seizures.

## What to do
1. Call 911 or Poison Control.
2. Stay with the person and keep them away from roads, water and heights.
3. Keep them cool.
4. Tell responders what was taken; hospitals can give physostigmine.
