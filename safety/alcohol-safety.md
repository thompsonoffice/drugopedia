# Alcohol safety

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice. No amounts, preparation, or sourcing. In an emergency in the US, call 911. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222. SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357.

## The biggest dangers

### Alcohol poisoning
Signs: confusion, vomiting, seizures, slow or irregular breathing, blue or pale skin, low temperature, and not waking up. Blood alcohol can keep rising after drinking stops.

### Mixing
Alcohol with opioids, benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, sleep drugs, or GHB greatly raises the risk of stopped breathing.

### Withdrawal
Daily heavy drinkers who stop suddenly can have seizures and delirium tremens. Stopping should be medically supervised.

### Methanol
Counterfeit or homemade spirits and some hand sanitizers can contain methanol, causing blindness and death.

## What to do
1. Call 911 for any sign of alcohol poisoning.
2. Do not let them "sleep it off" alone.
3. Recovery position on their side.
4. Do not give coffee or cold showers.
5. If opioids may be involved, give naloxone.
