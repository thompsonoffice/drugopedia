# Mescaline (peyote, San Pedro)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Phenethylamine psychedelic from cacti.

**Brand and street names:** Peyote (Lophophora williamsii), San Pedro (Echinopsis pachanoi), Peruvian torch; synthetic mescaline.

## History and origins

Peyote has been used in Mexico and the American Southwest for at least 5,000 years. Arthur Heffter isolated mescaline in 1897 and Ernst Späth synthesized it in 1919. Aldous Huxley's 'The Doors of Perception' (1954) described his experience. The Native American Church's peyote use is federally protected.

## Medical use, then and now

No approved use.

## How it entered recreational use

Used ceremonially and recreationally; peyote is slow-growing and threatened.

## Legal status

Mescaline and peyote are US Schedule I, with an exemption for Native American Church use; San Pedro cacti are legal to grow as ornamentals in many places. UN 1971 Schedule I (mescaline).

## What the products look and feel like

Peyote buttons are small, round, spineless, gray-green; San Pedro is a tall columnar cactus.

**Touch cannot confirm what a pill is.** Different manufacturers make the same medicine in different shapes and sizes, and counterfeit pills, such as fake 'M30' oxycodone 'blues' or fake Xanax 'bars', are pressed to imitate real ones and often contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines. Safer ways to check: ask a pharmacist (they can identify a pill from its imprint), use a pill-identifier app or a screen-reader-friendly service such as Be My Eyes or Seeing AI to read the imprint aloud and look it up on DailyMed or Drugs.com, keep medicines in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

Mescaline is long and gentle compared with LSD: warm, colorful, and grounded, often with deep empathy and a strong connection to nature.

Nausea and vomiting are very common at the start.

Physical risks are low but include raised heart rate and blood pressure.

Tolerance to classic psychedelics builds within a day and lasts several days, with cross-tolerance between them.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (45-90 min)

Mescaline, from the peyote cactus and San Pedro (Echinopsis) cacti, comes on slowly over an hour or two. The first stretch often brings nausea and vomiting from the intensely bitter taste, a heavy or cramping stomach, and a restless, stimulated body with tingling, warmth, yawning and goosebumps.

Mentally the onset feels like a gradual brightening and opening. Colors begin to glow, small details seem fascinating, and a sense of beauty and significance creeps in, often accompanied by a light, giggly mood.

Pupils widen, the heart rate rises, and some feel jittery, chilled or restless, with a slight tension in the jaw and a hollow, heavy stomach that lingers until vomiting passes.

Mescaline is absorbed slowly from the gut and needs much more than LSD to act, because it binds 5-HT2A weakly.

Nausea and vomiting occur early.

### Come-up (1-2 h)

Colors and emotions intensify.

### Peak (3-5 h)

At the peak colors are intensely vivid and saturated, often more so than with LSD; surfaces breathe and shimmer, and geometric patterns flow with eyes closed. The body feels warm and energized, sometimes heavy, with waves of tingling.

Emotionally the peak is often warm, empathetic and earthy, with a deep sense of connection to nature, other people and the land. Many say thought stays clearer and less chaotic than with LSD, though anxiety and difficult insights can also arise. Peyote is a sacrament in the Native American Church.

Psychedelics rarely threaten the body directly, but they impair judgment and coordination, so falls, traffic, water and heights are real dangers. Panic, paranoia and terrifying experiences can happen to anyone, and people with a personal or family history of psychosis or bipolar disorder face a higher risk of lasting harm.

At peak, 5-HT2A activation produces vivid colors and pattern visuals.

Heart rate and blood pressure rise modestly.

### Plateau (5-8 h)

The mescaline plateau is long, many hours of vivid, flowing visuals and reflective, unhurried thought. Colors stay intensely saturated, patterns ripple across surfaces, and the body feels warm, energized and often deeply connected to the ground, plants and open air. Stomach discomfort usually fades as the hours pass.

Mood is usually gentle and introspective, sometimes joyful and laughing, sometimes melancholy and moved to tears. Time seems to stretch, and people often feel drawn to nature, music or quiet conversation. Thinking tends to stay more coherent than with LSD, which many people find grounding.

Fatigue builds as the hours pass, and the long duration means people should plan to be in a safe, comfortable setting for most of a day, with no driving or responsibilities.

### Comedown (8-12 h)

The comedown is slow, with visuals softening into a gentle shimmer through the evening and a tired, contented body. Muscles may ache from the long day, and appetite often returns, though the stomach can stay delicate. Hydration and a light meal help.

Emotionally people often feel calm, tired and reflective, with a lingering sense of beauty and connection, and many want to sit quietly or talk over what they saw and felt. Some feel emotionally raw or tender.

Sleep can be difficult until it fully clears, sometimes well into the night. Peyote is slow-growing and threatened in the wild, and outside the Native American Church it is illegal in the US.

Half-life about 6 hours.

Afterglow and fatigue.

### After-effects (12-24 h)

Insomnia.

### Next day

Next-day tiredness.

### Lower vs higher doses (described without amounts)

At lower doses psychedelics bring brighter colors, mood changes and new ways of thinking. At higher doses, strong visual changes, loss of the usual sense of self, and time distortion occur. At very high levels, people may lose touch with reality entirely, and panic, confusion and risky behavior become more likely.

### Why the same amount affects people differently

Why the same amount affects people differently: body size, tolerance, genetics, mood and setting, health conditions, medicines (especially antidepressants and lithium), and product purity all change the effect.
