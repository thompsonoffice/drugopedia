# Lean (purple drank, sizzurp)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Any opioid can cause fatal overdose, and street drugs are frequently contaminated with fentanyl.

## At a glance

A mixture of prescription cough syrup containing codeine and promethazine (an antihistamine and anti-nausea drug), usually mixed with soda and hard candy.

**Brand and street names:** Street names: lean, purple drank, sizzurp, drank, barre, Texas tea. Source syrups historically included Actavis Promethazine with Codeine (discontinued by Actavis in 2014), Tussionex, and Phenergan with Codeine.

## History and origins

Lean grew out of Houston's Third Ward and South Side in the 1960s to 1980s among blues musicians and then rap artists. DJ Screw (Robert Earl Davis Jr.) created 'chopped and screwed' music in the early 1990s, slowed down to mirror the slow, heavy feeling of the drink, and his Screwed Up Click spread the culture. Songs like Three 6 Mafia's 'Sippin' on Some Syrup' (2000) took it national. DJ Screw died in 2000, Big Moe in 2007, and Pimp C of UGK in 2007, with codeine and promethazine implicated; Lil Wayne and others were hospitalized with seizures. Actavis stopped making its syrup in 2014 partly because of its fame in music.

## Medical use, then and now

The source syrups are prescribed for cough and cold symptoms. Lean itself has no medical use.

## How it entered recreational use

Lean is used socially, often sipped over hours from double-stacked foam cups. Counterfeit 'lean' made from other syrups, pills, or fentanyl-laced mixtures is now common.

## Legal status

Codeine-promethazine syrup is US Schedule V. Possessing it without a prescription is illegal.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

Lean is described as a slow, syrupy, heavy-bodied high, very different from codeine tablets alone. Promethazine adds deep sedation, a weighted, dreamy, slowed-down feeling, and a sense that time is dragging, which is the experience 'screwed' music was built to imitate. People describe feeling relaxed, 'leaned back', sleepy, and detached, with mild euphoria and itch from codeine.

Compared with plain codeine, lean feels sleepier, more sluggish, and more disorienting; speech slows, movements become clumsy, and many fall asleep wherever they are. Its sweet taste and sipping style mean people often keep drinking without noticing how sedated they are.

Tolerance changes everything here. Someone who has never used opioids feels far stronger effects, more nausea, and much higher overdose risk than someone who uses daily, and people who use every day often say they mainly feel 'normal' rather than high.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (20-45 min)

Sipped slowly, lean begins working in 20 to 45 minutes: a warm, heavy feeling in the limbs and a softening of the mind.

### Come-up (45-90 min)

The come-up is gradual. Promethazine's drowsiness and codeine's warmth build together; people describe their body getting heavy and their movements slowing down, with an itch on the face or chest.

### Peak (1-2 h)

The peak, around 1 to 2 hours, is a dreamy, deeply relaxed, almost underwater feeling. Speech and reactions slow, eyelids droop, and music and time feel stretched. Breathing slows, and with alcohol or pills this is when it becomes dangerous.

### Plateau (2-6 h)

The plateau can last several hours with continued sipping, with nodding, napping, and a 'stuck on the couch' feeling. Promethazine can cause restlessness in the legs, confusion, and in some people strange dreams or agitation.

### Comedown (4-8 h)

The comedown is drowsy and foggy; many simply sleep for many hours.

### After-effects (8-24 h)

After-effects include a heavy hangover, dry mouth, constipation, and grogginess. Promethazine can cause a lingering, restless, 'can't get comfortable' feeling.

### Next day

The next day is often sluggish. Regular lean use leads to codeine dependence, weight gain and tooth decay from the sugar, and withdrawal with insomnia, sweating, and irritability.

## Overdose note for Lean (purple drank, sizzurp)

Promethazine and codeine both depress breathing and add to each other. Adding alcohol, benzodiazepines, or Xanax bars is a common and deadly pattern. Promethazine can also cause seizures, dangerous heart rhythms, and, in children under 2, fatal breathing suppression. Counterfeit lean may contain fentanyl. People can stop breathing in their sleep.

Read the shared page: [What an opioid overdose feels and looks like, and what to do](../safety/opioid-overdose.md). Also see [naloxone (Narcan)](../narcan/naloxone.md).
