# 3-MMC (3-methylmethcathinone)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Synthetic cathinone, a positional isomer of mephedrone.

**Brand and street names:** 3-MMC, metaphedrone; often bought online in Europe.

## History and origins

3-MMC appeared in 2011 after mephedrone was banned, and became especially popular in the Netherlands and in European chemsex settings until it was controlled there in 2021.

## Medical use, then and now

None.

## How it entered recreational use

Popular for sociability and sexual arousal; often redosed through a night.

## Legal status

UN 1971 Convention Schedule II (2022); controlled across the EU; US analog laws apply.

## What the products look and feel like

White crystals or powder. Street and online products are frequently mislabeled; lab testing services have found products with different drugs or none of the advertised one.

**Touch cannot confirm what a pill is.** Different manufacturers make the same medicine in different shapes and sizes, and counterfeit pills, such as fake 'M30' oxycodone 'blues' or fake Xanax 'bars', are pressed to imitate real ones and often contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines. Safer ways to check: ask a pharmacist (they can identify a pill from its imprint), use a pill-identifier app or a screen-reader-friendly service such as Be My Eyes or Seeing AI to read the imprint aloud and look it up on DailyMed or Drugs.com, keep medicines in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

3-MMC is milder and more 'social' than mephedrone: talkativeness, a gentle euphoria, energy and arousal, with less intense rush.

Its short duration leads to frequent redosing, which brings a racing heart, sweating, insomnia and anxiety.

Heavy use is linked to compulsive use, kidney injury, paranoia and, rarely, deaths, particularly with other drugs.

Tolerance and compulsive redosing develop quickly with cathinones, and binges are common.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (15-45 min)

3-MMC has a quick onset of rushing euphoria, warmth and energy, with jaw clenching and a faster heart.

Mentally talkative, affectionate and euphoric, often compared to a mix of MDMA and cocaine.

Body temperature rises and sweating begins.

3-MMC releases dopamine and noradrenaline more than serotonin.

Heart rate and blood pressure rise.

### Come-up (30-60 min)

Sociability and energy rise.

### Peak (about 1 h)

At the peak, intense euphoria and stimulation that fades quickly, driving strong urges to re-dose.

Emotionally warm and open, then edgy.

Stimulants strain the heart: chest pain, a racing or irregular heartbeat, severe headache, very high body temperature, seizures or extreme agitation and paranoia are emergencies. Street stimulants are increasingly contaminated with fentanyl, so naloxone and test strips matter even for people who do not use opioids.

At peak, agitation and overheating are possible.

Repeated use is linked to compulsive redosing.

### Plateau (2-3 h)

The plateau is short.

Mood slides toward craving and anxiety.

Binges lead to sleeplessness and paranoia.

### Comedown (3-5 h)

The comedown brings exhaustion, low mood and anxiety.

Mood can be very low.

Dependence develops quickly.

Half-life is short.

Comedown brings craving.

### After-effects (6-12 h)

Insomnia and anxiety.

### Next day

Low mood and fatigue next day.

### Lower vs higher doses (described without amounts)

At lower doses cathinones bring energy, euphoria and sociability. At higher doses, agitation, a racing heart, sweating, paranoia and a strong urge to redose dominate. At very high levels, psychosis, overheating, seizures, muscle breakdown and heart failure can occur.

### Why the same amount affects people differently

Why the same amount affects people differently: tolerance, body size, genetics, heart health, mental health history, sleep, heat, hydration, and other drugs (including antidepressants and alcohol) all change the effect. Street products vary hugely in strength and content.

## Overdose note for 3-MMC (3-methylmethcathinone)

Toxicity causes overheating, fast heart rate, agitation and seizures.
