# Eutylone (bk-EBDB)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Synthetic cathinone with stimulant and MDMA-like effects.

**Brand and street names:** Eutylone, bk-EBDB; sold as 'molly', 'ecstasy' or bath salts.

## History and origins

Eutylone appeared on drug markets around 2014 and, after N-ethylpentylone was controlled, became the most common synthetic cathinone identified in US drug seizures and 'molly' samples around 2019 to 2021.

## Medical use, then and now

None.

## How it entered recreational use

Mostly taken unknowingly by people who believed they bought MDMA.

## Legal status

US Schedule I (temporary 2020, then permanent); UN 1971 Convention Schedule II (2022).

## What the products look and feel like

Off-white crystals or capsules indistinguishable from 'molly'. Street and online products are frequently mislabeled; lab testing services have found products with different drugs or none of the advertised one.

**Touch cannot confirm what a pill is.** Different manufacturers make the same medicine in different shapes and sizes, and counterfeit pills, such as fake 'M30' oxycodone 'blues' or fake Xanax 'bars', are pressed to imitate real ones and often contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines. Safer ways to check: ask a pharmacist (they can identify a pill from its imprint), use a pill-identifier app or a screen-reader-friendly service such as Be My Eyes or Seeing AI to read the imprint aloud and look it up on DailyMed or Drugs.com, keep medicines in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

Eutylone is more stimulating and less empathetic than MDMA: wired energy, racing thoughts and a short euphoria followed by a strong urge to take more.

Long-lasting insomnia, anxiety, sweating, teeth grinding and a racing heart are common; many describe it as 'MDMA that keeps you up all night without the love'.

Emergency reports describe agitation, overheating, seizures and deaths, often when people redosed because they thought the 'MDMA' was weak.

Tolerance and compulsive redosing develop quickly with cathinones, and binges are common.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (30-60 min)

Eutylone (bk-EBDB) became one of the most common substances found in fake 'Molly' and ecstasy around 2019–2021. Its onset is often deceptively mild: a slow rise of energy, a slight tingle and warmth, which leads people to think the drug is weak and take more.

Mentally the onset brings stimulation more than empathy: alertness, talkativeness and restlessness, with only a faint version of MDMA's warmth.

Heart rate climbs and the jaw tightens as it builds.

Eutylone blocks dopamine and noradrenaline reuptake and releases some serotonin.

Heart rate and blood pressure rise.

### Come-up (about 1 h)

Stimulation rises faster than euphoria.

### Peak (1-2 h)

At the peak eutylone produces stimulant euphoria and drive, with racing thoughts and a strong urge to keep going. Some feel a mild loving glow; many feel wired, sweaty and edgy.

Emotionally it can turn anxious or agitated, and insomnia is very common. People who expected MDMA are often disappointed and confused by the experience.

Stimulants strain the heart: chest pain, a racing or irregular heartbeat, severe headache, very high body temperature, seizures or extreme agitation and paranoia are emergencies. Street stimulants are increasingly contaminated with fentanyl, so naloxone and test strips matter even for people who do not use opioids. Re-dosing because it 'didn't work' is a key danger.

At peak, agitation, overheating and seizures are reported in emergency cases.

Toxicity often followed redosing.

### Plateau (3-6 h)

The plateau is long and wired, lasting much of the night.

Mood becomes restless, anxious and sometimes paranoid.

Sleep can be impossible for many hours.

### Comedown (6-12 h)

The comedown brings exhaustion, insomnia and jitters that can last into the next day.

Mood low, irritable and anxious.

Repeated heavy use is linked to psychosis, seizures and deaths.

Effects last longer than methylone.

Insomnia can last into the next night.

### After-effects (12-36 h)

Insomnia lasting into the next night.

### Next day (next 1-2 days)

Fatigue and low mood for a day or two.

### Lower vs higher doses (described without amounts)

At lower doses cathinones bring energy, euphoria and sociability. At higher doses, agitation, a racing heart, sweating, paranoia and a strong urge to redose dominate. At very high levels, psychosis, overheating, seizures, muscle breakdown and heart failure can occur.

### Why the same amount affects people differently

Why the same amount affects people differently: tolerance, body size, genetics, heart health, mental health history, sleep, heat, hydration, and other drugs (including antidepressants and alcohol) all change the effect. Street products vary hugely in strength and content.

## Overdose note for Eutylone (bk-EBDB)

Eutylone toxicity causes agitation, overheating, seizures, heart rhythm problems and death.
