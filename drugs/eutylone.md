# Eutylone (bk-EBDB)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Synthetic cathinone with stimulant and MDMA-like effects.

**Brand and street names:** Eutylone, bk-EBDB; sold as 'molly', 'ecstasy' or bath salts.

## History and origins

Eutylone appeared on drug markets around 2014 and, after N-ethylpentylone was controlled, became the most common synthetic cathinone identified in US drug seizures and 'molly' samples around 2019 to 2021.

## Medical use, then and now

None.

## How it entered recreational use

Mostly taken unknowingly by people who believed they bought MDMA.

## Legal status

US Schedule I (temporary 2020, then permanent); UN 1971 Convention Schedule II (2022).

## What the products look and feel like

Off-white crystals or capsules indistinguishable from 'molly'. Street and online products are frequently mislabeled; lab testing services have found products with different drugs or none of the advertised one.

**Touch cannot confirm what a pill is.** Different manufacturers make the same medicine in different shapes and sizes, and counterfeit pills, such as fake 'M30' oxycodone 'blues' or fake Xanax 'bars', are pressed to imitate real ones and often contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines. Safer ways to check: ask a pharmacist (they can identify a pill from its imprint), use a pill-identifier app or a screen-reader-friendly service such as Be My Eyes or Seeing AI to read the imprint aloud and look it up on DailyMed or Drugs.com, keep medicines in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

Eutylone is more stimulating and less empathetic than MDMA: wired energy, racing thoughts and a short euphoria followed by a strong urge to take more.

Long-lasting insomnia, anxiety, sweating, teeth grinding and a racing heart are common; many describe it as 'MDMA that keeps you up all night without the love'.

Emergency reports describe agitation, overheating, seizures and deaths, often when people redosed because they thought the 'MDMA' was weak.

Tolerance and compulsive redosing develop quickly with cathinones, and binges are common.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (30-60 min)

Eutylone (bk-EBDB) is a cathinone that, from around 2019 to 2021, was one of the most common substances found in pills and powders sold as MDMA in the US. Swallowed, it comes on over a short while with a stimulating rush, faster heart, wide pupils and a clenched jaw. Some feel nauseated early and notice cold hands and feet. Some feel their pulse in their temples.

Mentally the onset is an energetic, talkative euphoria with some warmth, but less of MDMA's emotional openness; many describe it as 'speedy' and less satisfying.

Because buyers usually expected MDMA, the weaker emotional effect often led to redosing, which drives insomnia, overheating and anxiety.

Eutylone blocks dopamine and noradrenaline reuptake and releases some serotonin.

Heart rate and blood pressure rise.

### Come-up (about 1 h)

Stimulation rises faster than euphoria.

### Peak (1-2 h)

At its peak the body is wired and hot, with sweating, teeth grinding, a pounding pulse and restlessness that makes it hard to sit still. Body temperature climbs, and dancing in hot rooms adds to overheating; muscles may twitch. The jaw may ache from clenching, and the mouth becomes very dry.

Mentally there is stimulation and some euphoria mixed with jittery nervousness; long-lasting wakefulness is typical. Many people feel unsettled that it does not feel like they expected, and anxiety grows. Some people pace or fidget constantly and can't finish a conversation.

Agitation, paranoia, seizures, overheating and heart problems have been reported, especially at higher amounts or with redosing.

At peak, agitation, overheating and seizures are reported in emergency cases.

Toxicity often followed redosing.

### Plateau (3-6 h)

The plateau is long and stimulating, with wakefulness that lasts well past the pleasant effects. The body stays tense, dehydrated and unable to rest. Jaw clenching continues, the mouth stays dry and heart rate remains high hours later. Some feel cold and shaky despite the sweating.

Mentally the warmth fades into anxious alertness, racing thoughts and irritability; some people become paranoid or confused after many hours awake. Some people hear things or feel watched late in the night. Thoughts may race from topic to topic without settling.

Sleep may be impossible for many hours, which greatly worsens the next day. Alcohol or other stimulants taken to 'boost' it add danger.

### Comedown (6-12 h)

The comedown brings exhaustion, soreness, headache and broken sleep. Some feel achy, headachy and feverish-seeming the next day, with poor appetite. Muscles may feel tight and sore and the jaw tender, and eating may feel like a chore.

Emotionally low mood, anxiety and irritability can last days. Concentration and memory can feel foggy for a day or two. Some feel teary, irritable or anxious without clear reason.

Drug-checking services and test kits can help people identify eutylone in supposed MDMA and avoid unwanted effects. Avoid redosing and mixing with other drugs, and rest and rehydrate afterward. Drug-checking services can confirm what a pill contains.

Effects last longer than methylone.

Insomnia can last into the next night.

### After-effects (12-36 h)

Insomnia lasting into the next night.

### Next day (next 1-2 days)

Fatigue and low mood for a day or two.

### Lower vs higher doses (described without amounts)

At lower doses cathinones bring energy, euphoria and sociability. At higher doses, agitation, a racing heart, sweating, paranoia and a strong urge to redose dominate. At very high levels, psychosis, overheating, seizures, muscle breakdown and heart failure can occur.

### Why the same amount affects people differently

Why the same amount affects people differently: tolerance, body size, genetics, heart health, mental health history, sleep, heat, hydration, and other drugs (including antidepressants and alcohol) all change the effect. Street products vary hugely in strength and content.

## Overdose note for Eutylone (bk-EBDB)

Eutylone toxicity causes agitation, overheating, seizures, heart rhythm problems and death.
