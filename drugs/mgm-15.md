# MGM-15 and mitragynine pseudoindoxyl

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Any opioid can cause fatal overdose, and street drugs are frequently contaminated with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Semi-synthetic derivatives related to kratom alkaloids. Mitragynine pseudoindoxyl is a rearrangement product of 7-OH; MGM-15 is a name used for a research-derived kratom analog sold in some online and smoke-shop products.

**Brand and street names:** MGM-15 and 'pseudo' products; often mislabeled.

## History and origins

Mitragynine pseudoindoxyl was described in research in the 2010s (Váradi et al., 2016) as a potent mu agonist with less breathing suppression in mice. MGM-15 and similar compounds appeared on the US gray market in 2024 to 2025.

## Medical use, then and now

None.

## How it entered recreational use

Marketed to people who use kratom or 7-OH products.

## Legal status

Uncontrolled federally in most cases but covered by some state bans; legal status is changing quickly.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

There is almost no reliable information about what these compounds feel like in humans. Reports describe opioid-like warmth, sedation, and euphoria, sometimes said to be very strong.

Animal research claims of 'safer' breathing effects have not been tested in people, and products are unregulated and often mislabeled.

Tolerance changes everything here. Someone who has never used opioids feels far stronger effects, more nausea, and much higher overdose risk than someone who uses daily, and people who use every day often say they mainly feel 'normal' rather than high.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (15-45 min)

Effects reportedly begin within 15 to 45 minutes when swallowed.

### Come-up (30-60 min)

Opioid-like warmth builds.

### Peak (1-2 h)

Peak reported as strong opioid-like euphoria.

### Plateau (2-4 h)

Duration unknown, reported as several hours.

### Comedown (4-6 h)

Fades gradually.

### After-effects (6-24 h)

Unknown.

### Next day

Dependence and withdrawal are likely.

## Overdose note for MGM-15 and mitragynine pseudoindoxyl

Unknown potency and purity. Treat as a strong opioid; naloxone is expected to reverse it.
