# 5-MeO-DMT

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Very potent, short-acting tryptamine; found in some plants and the venom gland secretion of the Sonoran Desert toad.

**Brand and street names:** 5-MeO, 'toad', Bufo alvarius (Incilius alvarius) secretion; GH001 (in trials).

## History and origins

First synthesized in 1936 and identified in plants in 1959. Use of toad secretion was popularized in the 1980s and rose in the 2010s through retreats; wild toads have suffered from collection.

## Medical use, then and now

No approved use; GH001 is in trials for depression.

## How it entered recreational use

Used in retreats for intense 'non-dual' experiences.

## Legal status

US Schedule I (2011).

## What the products look and feel like

Synthetic 5-MeO-DMT is a white powder; toad secretion dries to a crystalline film. Toad secretion also contains bufotoxins, which affect the heart. Street and online products are frequently mislabeled; lab testing services have found products with different drugs or none of the advertised one.

**Touch cannot confirm what a pill is.** Different manufacturers make the same medicine in different shapes and sizes, and counterfeit pills, such as fake 'M30' oxycodone 'blues' or fake Xanax 'bars', are pressed to imitate real ones and often contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines. Safer ways to check: ask a pharmacist (they can identify a pill from its imprint), use a pill-identifier app or a screen-reader-friendly service such as Be My Eyes or Seeing AI to read the imprint aloud and look it up on DailyMed or Drugs.com, keep medicines in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

5-MeO-DMT is less visual than DMT: an overwhelming dissolution of self, sometimes described as white light or 'nothingness'.

People may become unresponsive, thrash, or vomit; choking and falls are dangers.

Deaths have been reported at ceremonies, and combining with MAOIs is especially dangerous.

Tolerance to classic psychedelics builds within a day and lasts several days, with cross-tolerance between them.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the inhaled and smoked route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (seconds)

5-MeO-DMT, from certain plants and the toad Incilius alvarius, is very fast and very different from DMT. Within seconds, a powerful rush of pressure and vibration sweeps through the body, and the sense of having a body can vanish.

Mentally the onset is often described as total dissolution: ego, thoughts, time and space disappear into a white light or void. There are usually few colorful visuals.

People may cry out, thrash, vomit or become unresponsive without knowing it.

Inhaled 5-MeO-DMT reaches the brain within seconds.

Heart rate and blood pressure rise; muscle movements may be involuntary.

### Come-up (under 1 min)

Rapid loss of contact with surroundings.

### Peak (1-5 min)

At the peak many report complete 'non-dual' unity, a sense of being everything and nothing at once.

Emotionally it can be blissful or utterly terrifying.

The physical risks are higher than DMT: seizures, vomiting while unaware, and dangerous interactions, especially with MAO inhibitors. Deaths have occurred. Psychedelics rarely threaten the body directly, but they impair judgment and coordination, so falls, traffic, water and heights are real dangers. Panic, paranoia and terrifying experiences can happen to anyone, and people with a personal or family history of psychosis or bipolar disorder face a higher risk of lasting harm.

At peak, strong 5-HT1A and 5-HT2A activation produces loss of awareness; people may stop responding entirely.

Combined with MAO inhibitors (including ayahuasca), 5-MeO-DMT has caused fatal serotonin toxicity.

### Plateau (5-15 min)

The plateau is short, and sensory awareness returns over minutes.

Mentally people return disoriented and shaken.

A sober sitter is essential.

### Comedown (15-30 min)

The comedown may bring euphoria or lingering anxiety.

Some experience 'reactivations' of the state in the following days.

No physical dependence develops.

Rapid breakdown by MAO ends effects in under an hour.

Some people experience 'reactivations' or anxiety for days after.

### After-effects (hours)

Emotional afterglow or distress.

### Next day (none)

Usually none; some report lingering anxiety.

### Lower vs higher doses (described without amounts)

At lower doses psychedelics bring brighter colors, mood changes and new ways of thinking. At higher doses, strong visual changes, loss of the usual sense of self, and time distortion occur. At very high levels, people may lose touch with reality entirely, and panic, confusion and risky behavior become more likely.

### Why the same amount affects people differently

Why the same amount affects people differently: body size, tolerance, genetics, mood and setting, health conditions, medicines (especially antidepressants and lithium), and product purity all change the effect.

## Overdose note for 5-MeO-DMT

Serious risks include breathing problems, seizures, choking and serotonin syndrome with MAOIs.
