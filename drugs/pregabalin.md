# Pregabalin (Lyrica)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Mixing depressants (alcohol, opioids, benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, sleep drugs, muscle relaxants) is the leading cause of deaths with these drugs. Some must never be stopped suddenly after daily use.

## At a glance

Gabapentinoid (alpha-2-delta ligand), more potent and faster than gabapentin.

**Brand and street names:** Lyrica, Lyrica CR. Street: 'buds', 'lyrics'.

## History and origins

Developed by Richard Silverman at Northwestern University and approved in 2004; Pfizer's Lyrica became a blockbuster.

## Medical use, then and now

Nerve pain from diabetes and shingles, fibromyalgia, spinal cord injury pain, seizures; approved for generalized anxiety disorder in Europe.

## How it entered recreational use

Misused for euphoria and relaxation, especially combined with opioids; a significant problem in Scandinavia, the UK and Scotland.

## Legal status

US Schedule V. UK Class C (2019).

## Immediate-release vs extended-release

Immediate-release pregabalin is taken two or three times daily. Lyrica CR is taken once daily after an evening meal, giving steadier levels; it must be swallowed whole.

## What the pills feel like

Lyrica capsules are hard capsules in different colors by strength. Per DailyMed, Lyrica CR tablets are film-coated and almond-shaped (light blue, beige, or rose by strength), debossed 'VLE' on one side and 'PGN' with the strength number on the other.

**Touch cannot confirm what a pill is.** Different manufacturers make the same medicine in different shapes and sizes, and counterfeit pills, such as fake 'M30' oxycodone 'blues' or fake Xanax 'bars', are pressed to imitate real ones and often contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines. Safer ways to check: ask a pharmacist (they can identify a pill from its imprint), use a pill-identifier app or a screen-reader-friendly service such as Be My Eyes or Seeing AI to read the imprint aloud and look it up on DailyMed or Drugs.com, keep medicines in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

Pregabalin's character is a warm, drunk-like euphoria and calm, with loosened anxiety, dizziness, and unsteadiness, stronger and faster than gabapentin.

Some describe a pleasant, sociable buzz; others feel only dizzy and tired. Blurred vision and weight gain are common with regular use.

Combined with opioids or alcohol, breathing can slow dangerously.

Tolerance to the pleasant and sleep effects builds with regular use, but tolerance to slowed breathing builds more slowly, so the danger stays. Daily use can lead to dependence, and stopping suddenly can be dangerous.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (30-90 min)

Pregabalin (Lyrica) is absorbed faster and more reliably than gabapentin, and the onset is smoother and stronger. Warmth and heaviness spread through the body, muscles loosen, and a light, floaty dizziness arrives.

Mentally pregabalin can bring a clear, warm euphoria and calm. People often describe it as alcohol-like: sociable, confident, chatty and relaxed, with anxiety switched off.

Blurry vision and unsteadiness show up early.

Medically, the onset reflects slow and variable absorption. Pregabalin is absorbed fully and quickly and cleared unchanged by the kidneys with a half-life of about 6 hours. Gabapentin is absorbed by a transporter in the gut that saturates, so more is not absorbed in proportion; pregabalin is absorbed more fully and quickly.

Early effects include calm, mild dizziness, and relaxation. Because gabapentinoids are cleared by the kidneys, people with kidney disease or older adults are affected more strongly and for longer.

### Come-up (1 h)

Lightness, warmth, dizziness.

### Peak (1-3 h)

At the peak the body feels warm, loose and pleasantly drunk, with clumsy movements and slurred speech. Some report a tingling glow on the skin.

Emotionally the peak is relaxed, giddy and uninhibited; with more, it becomes confused and sleepy.

Combined with opioids, pregabalin greatly raises the risk of fatal breathing suppression. Swelling of the face or throat is a rare allergic emergency.

Medically, the peak brings the strongest dizziness, unsteadiness, and sedation. Some people feel calm sociability or mild euphoria; others feel only clumsy and sleepy.

In 2019 the FDA warned that gabapentinoids can cause serious breathing problems when combined with opioids or other depressants, or in people with lung disease or older age. Gabapentinoids appear in many opioid overdose deaths.

### Plateau (3-6 h)

The plateau is a long, mellow, sedated glow.

Memory and concentration are poor.

Weight gain and swelling can occur with long use.

### Comedown (6-12 h total)

The comedown brings tiredness and sometimes headache.

Mood can drop, with anxiety or irritability.

Regular use can cause dependence, and stopping suddenly can trigger anxiety, insomnia, nausea, sweating and seizures.

As levels fall, dizziness and sedation fade over several hours. Sleep is often deep.

Daily use can cause dependence; stopping suddenly can cause anxiety, insomnia, sweating, pain, agitation, and occasionally seizures, so medical tapering is advised.

### After-effects (12-24 h)

Tiredness, blurred vision.

### Next day (little)

Little next-day effect.

### Lower vs higher doses (described without amounts)

At lower doses, gabapentinoids cause calm, mild dizziness, and relaxation. At higher doses, unsteadiness, slurred speech, double vision, and heavy sedation increase, and some people feel a drunk-like euphoria. With opioids or other depressants, breathing can slow dangerously.

### Why the same amount affects people differently

Why the same amount affects people differently: tolerance, body size, age, liver and kidney function, genetics, and especially other depressants (alcohol, opioids, benzodiazepines, gabapentinoids, antihistamines) change the effect. Older adults and people with sleep apnea or lung disease are at higher risk. Stronger effects do not stop at more pleasure: they shade into blackouts, falls, and slowed breathing.

## Overdose note for Pregabalin (Lyrica)

Overdose causes heavy drowsiness, confusion and sometimes seizures; with opioids it can stop breathing.

Read the shared page: [Mixing depressants: why combinations kill and what to do](../safety/depressant-mixing.md).
