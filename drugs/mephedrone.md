# Mephedrone (4-MMC)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Synthetic cathinone releasing dopamine and serotonin.

**Brand and street names:** Meow meow, M-CAT, drone, bubble.

## History and origins

First described in 1929, it became popular in the UK from about 2007 as a legal high and was banned there in 2010.

## Medical use, then and now

None.

## How it entered recreational use

Popular in clubs as an MDMA-like stimulant; injected in some chemsex scenes.

## Legal status

US Schedule I (2012); UN 1971 Schedule II (2015).

## What the products look and feel like

White or off-white powder or crystals, sometimes with a fishy smell. Street and online products are frequently mislabeled; lab testing services have found products with different drugs or none of the advertised one.

**Touch cannot confirm what a pill is.** Different manufacturers make the same medicine in different shapes and sizes, and counterfeit pills, such as fake 'M30' oxycodone 'blues' or fake Xanax 'bars', are pressed to imitate real ones and often contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines. Safer ways to check: ask a pharmacist (they can identify a pill from its imprint), use a pill-identifier app or a screen-reader-friendly service such as Be My Eyes or Seeing AI to read the imprint aloud and look it up on DailyMed or Drugs.com, keep medicines in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

Mephedrone feels like a mix of MDMA and cocaine: euphoria, empathy, talkativeness and energy, but short-lived, with a strong urge to redose.

Users report sweating, a pounding heart, jaw clenching and cold or bluish fingers from narrowed blood vessels.

Heavy use causes paranoia and insomnia, and injecting causes abscesses and infections.

Tolerance and compulsive redosing develop quickly with cathinones, and binges are common.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the intranasal (nasal sprays and snorting) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (10-20 min)

Snorted, effects start within 10 to 20 minutes.

Medically, the onset depends on route. Mephedrone releases dopamine and serotonin. Cathinones are beta-keto relatives of amphetamine; many cross into the brain quickly, causing a strong rush of energy and euphoria.

Early signs are a racing heart, sweating, jaw clenching and enlarged pupils.

### Come-up (15-30 min)

A fast rise of euphoria.

### Peak (30-60 min)

Peak within 30 to 60 minutes.

Medically, the peak brings the highest risk of overheating, dangerous heart rhythms, high blood pressure, agitation and psychosis ('excited delirium' in emergency reports). Strong compulsion to redose is typical.

Severe cases cause muscle breakdown (rhabdomyolysis), kidney failure, and death. Products sold as 'bath salts' or as 'MDMA' often contain unknown cathinones.

### Plateau (1-2 h)

Effects last 1 to 2 hours.

### Comedown (2-4 h)

Comedown with craving.

As levels fall, people crash hard, with anxiety, low mood, insomnia and cravings.

Binges can last days without sleep, leading to paranoia, hallucinations and long recovery times.

### After-effects (4-12 h)

Anxiety and insomnia.

### Next day

Low mood for a day or two.

### Lower vs higher doses (described without amounts)

At lower doses cathinones bring energy, euphoria and sociability. At higher doses, agitation, a racing heart, sweating, paranoia and a strong urge to redose dominate. At very high levels, psychosis, overheating, seizures, muscle breakdown and heart failure can occur.

### Why the same amount affects people differently

Why the same amount affects people differently: tolerance, body size, genetics, heart health, mental health history, sleep, heat, hydration, and other drugs (including antidepressants and alcohol) all change the effect. Street products vary hugely in strength and content.
