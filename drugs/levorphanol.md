# Levorphanol

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Any opioid can cause fatal overdose, and street drugs are frequently contaminated with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Synthetic morphinan opioid; mu, kappa, and delta agonist, NMDA antagonist, and serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor.

**Brand and street names:** Levo-Dromoran (original brand), generic levorphanol tartrate.

## History and origins

First described in Germany in 1946 and introduced in the US in 1953 by Roche. It fell out of favor for decades and was rediscovered for difficult nerve pain.

## Medical use, then and now

Moderate to severe pain, including nerve pain, often when other opioids have failed. Long-acting and can accumulate.

## How it entered recreational use

Rarely misused, mostly because it is rarely prescribed.

## Legal status

US Schedule II.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

Levorphanol is described as a long, smooth, clear-headed opioid effect with less euphoria than oxycodone and a slightly 'different', sometimes dreamy or dysphoric edge from its kappa activity. Some report odd perceptual effects at higher levels.

Its long half-life gives a steady, drawn-out feeling, more like methadone than oxycodone.

Tolerance changes everything here. Someone who has never used opioids feels far stronger effects, more nausea, and much higher overdose risk than someone who uses daily, and people who use every day often say they mainly feel 'normal' rather than high.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (30-60 min)

Swallowed, it begins in about 30 to 60 minutes with a gradual warmth.

### Come-up (1-2 h)

The come-up is slow and even, with relaxation and easing of pain.

### Peak (1-2 h)

The peak, around 1 to 2 hours, is a calm, moderate euphoria with some sedation; some people feel slightly detached.

### Plateau (6-15 h)

The plateau lasts many hours, often 6 to 15.

### Comedown (15-24 h)

The comedown is slow.

### After-effects (1-2 days)

Accumulation with repeated doses can cause more sedation over days.

### Next day (still active)

After regular use, withdrawal is slow-onset and long.

## Overdose note for Levorphanol

Long half-life means accumulation and delayed overdose, similar to methadone.

Read the shared page: [What an opioid overdose feels and looks like, and what to do](../safety/opioid-overdose.md).
