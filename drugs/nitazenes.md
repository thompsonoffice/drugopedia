# Nitazenes (isotonitazene, protonitazene, metonitazene, and others)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Any opioid can cause fatal overdose, and street drugs are frequently contaminated with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Benzimidazole opioids, a chemical family unrelated to fentanyl. Many are stronger than fentanyl.

**Brand and street names:** Isotonitazene ('iso'), protonitazene, metonitazene, etonitazene, etodesnitazene, N-pyrrolidino etonitazene, and others.

## History and origins

Developed by the Swiss company CIBA in the late 1950s as possible painkillers; never approved because of their danger. Isotonitazene appeared on the illicit market in 2019, followed by dozens of relatives, especially in the UK, Europe, and the US. They are found in counterfeit oxycodone and benzo pills and in heroin.

## Medical use, then and now

None.

## How it entered recreational use

Usually taken unknowingly in pills or powders sold as other drugs.

## Legal status

Isotonitazene and many relatives US Schedule I; UK Class A since 2024.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

Little is known from direct reports. People describe a heavy, fast opioid effect, similar to or stronger than fentanyl, with rapid loss of consciousness.

Because they are in counterfeit pills, most people never know they took a nitazene.

Tolerance changes everything here. Someone who has never used opioids feels far stronger effects, more nausea, and much higher overdose risk than someone who uses daily, and people who use every day often say they mainly feel 'normal' rather than high.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (15-45 min)

Onset within seconds to minutes depending on route.

### Come-up (30-60 min)

Little come-up.

### Peak (1-2 h)

Heavy sedation, sometimes immediate unconsciousness.

### Plateau (2-4 h)

Duration unclear, may be longer than fentanyl.

### Comedown (4-6 h)

Comedown unclear.

### After-effects (6-24 h)

Unknown.

### Next day

Strong dependence expected.

## Overdose note for Nitazenes (isotonitazene, protonitazene, metonitazene, and others)

Extremely potent; overdose can happen quickly and may need several doses of naloxone. Standard fentanyl test strips do not detect them; nitazene test strips exist.

Read the shared page: [What an opioid overdose feels and looks like, and what to do](../safety/opioid-overdose.md). Also see [Xylazine (tranq)](../safety/xylazine.md). Also see [naloxone (Narcan)](../narcan/naloxone.md).
