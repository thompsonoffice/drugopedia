# Research-chemical tryptamines (4-AcO-DMT, 4-HO-MET, AMT, 5-MeO-MiPT, DPT)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Synthetic tryptamines related to psilocybin and DMT.

**Brand and street names:** 4-AcO-DMT (psilacetin), 4-HO-MET, AMT, 5-MeO-MiPT (moxy), DPT, 5-MeO-DALT.

## History and origins

Many were described by Shulgin in TiHKAL (1997) and sold online from the 2000s.

## Medical use, then and now

None.

## How it entered recreational use

Sold online as 'legal mushrooms' and research chemicals.

## Legal status

AMT is US Schedule I; others vary or fall under analog laws.

## What the products look and feel like

Powders or capsules. Street and online products are frequently mislabeled; lab testing services have found products with different drugs or none of the advertised one.

**Touch cannot confirm what a pill is.** Different manufacturers make the same medicine in different shapes and sizes, and counterfeit pills, such as fake 'M30' oxycodone 'blues' or fake Xanax 'bars', are pressed to imitate real ones and often contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines. Safer ways to check: ask a pharmacist (they can identify a pill from its imprint), use a pill-identifier app or a screen-reader-friendly service such as Be My Eyes or Seeing AI to read the imprint aloud and look it up on DailyMed or Drugs.com, keep medicines in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

4-AcO-DMT feels much like psilocybin; 4-HO-MET is more visual and lighter emotionally.

AMT is long and stimulating and also inhibits MAO, raising interaction risk; 5-MeO-MiPT is tactile and less visual.

Deaths have been linked to AMT and 5-MeO tryptamines combined with other drugs.

Tolerance to classic psychedelics builds within a day and lasts several days, with cross-tolerance between them.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (20 min-3 h)

Research tryptamines like 4-AcO-DMT ('synthetic shrooms'), 4-HO-MET, 4-HO-MiPT, DPT and 5-MeO-MiPT vary. The onset often brings mild nausea, yawning, a slight chill and a tingly energy. Research tryptamines such as 4-AcO-DMT, 4-HO-MET, 4-HO-MiPT, 5-MeO-MiPT, DPT and AMT differ greatly in onset: 4-substituted compounds feel like psilocybin and arrive within an hour, while AMT can take hours. The body often feels warm, tingly or queasy, with yawning, chills and widening pupils.

Mentally a gradual opening and brightening, with giggles or anticipation. Mood may lift into giggling or tighten into anxious anticipation.

Many 4-substituted ones feel similar to psilocybin; others are more stimulating or bodily.

Absorption varies; 4-AcO-DMT converts to psilocin.

Nausea is common.

### Come-up (1-2 h)

Body load and visuals build.

### Peak (2-6 h)

At the peak there are visuals and altered thinking; 4-HO-MET is known as colorful and lighthearted, DPT as intense and ritualistic, 5-MeO-MiPT as tactile and sensual. Body load varies from light to heavy. Visuals can be strong, with breathing surfaces and vivid closed-eye imagery.

Emotionally euphoric or difficult, like other psychedelics.

Psychedelics rarely threaten the body directly, but they impair judgment and coordination, so falls, traffic, water and heights are real dangers. Panic, paranoia and terrifying experiences can happen to anyone, and people with a personal or family history of psychosis or bipolar disorder face a higher risk of lasting harm.

At peak, psychedelic effects; AMT adds stimulation.

AMT's MAO inhibition can cause serotonin syndrome with other drugs.

### Plateau (2-8 h)

The plateau depends on the compound. 4-AcO-DMT and 4-HO-MET give a few hours of steady psilocybin-like visuals and warm body sensations; 4-HO-MET is described as lighter and more visual, 4-HO-MiPT as tactile and colorful. AMT's plateau can last most of a day, with strong stimulation, muscle tension and stomach discomfort.

Mood ranges from playful and emotionally open to introspective or overwhelmed, and thought can loop. 5-MeO-MiPT is often described as more tactile and sensual with fewer visuals, while DPT can be intensely spiritual or frightening.

Overheating, agitation and serotonin toxicity are risks, especially for AMT, which also acts as an MAO inhibitor and is dangerous with antidepressants, MDMA or stimulants. Product identity is often uncertain.

### Comedown (4-16 h)

The comedown is gradual, from gentle and short for 4-substituted compounds to long and draining for AMT. The body feels tired, achy and sometimes tense, with a lingering upset stomach or headache. Faint visuals may linger, and the skin can feel tingly or oversensitive for a while.

Mood is often reflective and calm, though anxiety, flatness or irritability can follow intense or long experiences. Many people feel emotionally tender and want quiet, food and rest.

Sleep may be delayed for hours, especially with AMT. Tolerance builds quickly and is shared with other psychedelics, and mixing compounds or redosing early raises the risk of a difficult trip.

Durations range from a few hours to over a day.

Insomnia and fatigue follow.

### After-effects (12-24 h)

Fatigue.

### Next day

AMT may last into next day.

### Lower vs higher doses (described without amounts)

At lower doses psychedelics bring brighter colors, mood changes and new ways of thinking. At higher doses, strong visual changes, loss of the usual sense of self, and time distortion occur. At very high levels, people may lose touch with reality entirely, and panic, confusion and risky behavior become more likely.

### Why the same amount affects people differently

Why the same amount affects people differently: body size, tolerance, genetics, mood and setting, health conditions, medicines (especially antidepressants and lithium), and product purity all change the effect.

## Overdose note for Research-chemical tryptamines (4-AcO-DMT, 4-HO-MET, AMT, 5-MeO-MiPT, DPT)

Risks include overheating, serotonin syndrome (especially AMT with other drugs), and accidents.
