# Amphetamine (Adderall, Dexedrine, Evekeo)

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Street products are often mislabeled or contaminated, including with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Releases dopamine and noradrenaline; central nervous system stimulant.

**Brand and street names:** Adderall and Adderall XR (mixed amphetamine salts), Mydayis, Dexedrine and Zenzedi (dextroamphetamine), Evekeo, Adzenys. Street: addies, speed, uppers.

## History and origins

First synthesized by Lazăr Edeleanu in 1887. Gordon Alles described its effects in 1929, and Smith, Kline & French sold the Benzedrine inhaler from 1933. Soldiers in World War II were given amphetamines. Adderall, originally the diet drug Obetrol, was approved for ADHD in 1996.

## Medical use, then and now

Historically for nasal congestion, depression, narcolepsy and weight loss. Today: ADHD and narcolepsy.

## How it entered recreational use

Students and workers misuse it for focus and staying awake; it is also used for euphoria. Counterfeit 'Adderall' made with methamphetamine is common.

## Legal status

US Schedule II; UN 1971 Convention Schedule II.

## Immediate-release vs extended-release

Immediate-release amphetamine (Adderall, Evekeo, Zenzedi) works for about 4 to 6 hours. Extended-release forms (Adderall XR beads, Mydayis, Adzenys XR-ODT, Dyanavel XR) release drug in stages over about 10 to 16 hours, with a smoother rise and fall. They are designed to be taken exactly as directed; damaging them releases more drug at once and is dangerous.

## What the pills feel like

Per DailyMed, brand Adderall tablets are blue (lowest strength) or orange (higher strengths), round or oval, scored, and debossed 'dp' with the strength number. Adderall XR capsules have blue or orange bodies. Counterfeit Adderall made with methamphetamine has caused deaths. Stimulant drugs sold on the street, including counterfeit Adderall, are often contaminated with methamphetamine or fentanyl.

**Touch cannot confirm what a pill is.** Different manufacturers make the same medicine in different shapes and sizes, and counterfeit pills, such as fake 'M30' oxycodone 'blues' or fake Xanax 'bars', are pressed to imitate real ones and often contain fentanyl or designer benzodiazepines. Safer ways to check: ask a pharmacist (they can identify a pill from its imprint), use a pill-identifier app or a screen-reader-friendly service such as Be My Eyes or Seeing AI to read the imprint aloud and look it up on DailyMed or Drugs.com, keep medicines in labeled pharmacy bottles, and use fentanyl test strips on anything not from a pharmacy.

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

Amphetamine's character is driven focus: energy, confidence, talkativeness and narrowed attention, with less hunger and need for sleep.

At higher levels, euphoria grows along with a racing heart, jaw clenching, sweating and anxiety; people may stay awake for long periods.

The comedown brings irritability, low mood, fatigue and hunger. Repeated high use can cause paranoia, psychosis and heart damage.

Tolerance to the euphoria builds quickly, while the heart and blood-pressure effects remain. Regular users often chase the first high and use more often.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (30-60 min)

Amphetamine (Adderall, Dexedrine) by mouth comes on gradually. People first notice a gentle wakefulness, a slightly faster heartbeat, a dry mouth and a loss of appetite. Some feel a mild jitteriness or a slight churning in the stomach.

Mentally, the onset brings clarity and motivation. Tasks that felt dull become absorbing, distractions fade, and confidence rises. People with ADHD often describe feeling calmer and more organized rather than high.

Pupils widen slightly, the jaw may tense, and body temperature rises a little. Extended-release forms build more slowly and less noticeably than immediate-release ones.

Amphetamine is well absorbed from the gut; dextroamphetamine is the more potent form centrally, and levoamphetamine adds more peripheral effects.

It reverses dopamine and noradrenaline transporters, pushing these chemicals out of neurons, which raises heart rate and blood pressure early.

### Come-up (1-2 h)

Focus and energy build; appetite fades.

### Peak (2-3 h)

At the peak, the body feels energized and warm, with a faster pulse, sometimes sweaty palms, and tension in the jaw and neck. Food is unappealing, and the bladder can feel slow.

When used as prescribed, the emotional tone is focused and steady. Misused at high amounts, it becomes euphoric, chatty, confident and restless, with racing thoughts and a strong urge to keep busy. Anxiety and irritability can surface.

Misuse raises blood pressure and heart rate and can cause chest pain, heart rhythm problems, overheating and, rarely, psychosis. Counterfeit 'Adderall' pills may contain methamphetamine or fentanyl.

At peak, dopamine in the striatum and prefrontal cortex is high; at therapeutic levels this improves focus, but at high levels it causes euphoria, stereotyped behavior and psychosis.

Cardiovascular risks include high blood pressure, arrhythmias and, rarely, stroke; labels carry a boxed warning for misuse and dependence.

### Plateau (3-6 h, XR 8-12 h)

The plateau sustains energy and focus for hours, much longer with extended-release capsules. The mouth stays dry and appetite stays low.

Mentally people feel productive, talkative or fixated; some get stuck on low-value tasks. Time can seem to pass quickly.

Sleep becomes difficult if the plateau extends into the evening.

### Comedown (6-12 h)

The comedown brings fatigue and heaviness, sometimes a headache and sudden hunger.

Emotionally irritability, flat mood, anxiety or sadness are common, often called the 'Adderall crash'.

Heavy or regular misuse leads to tolerance and dependence, with long crashes, poor sleep and low mood when stopping.

Half-life is about 10 to 12 hours, longer if urine is alkaline.

Insomnia and appetite loss persist; heavy misuse causes depression during withdrawal.

### After-effects (12-24 h)

Trouble sleeping, low appetite.

### Next day

Next-day fatigue and low mood after heavy use.

### Lower vs higher doses (described without amounts)

At lower doses stimulants increase alertness, focus, energy and confidence and reduce appetite. At higher doses euphoria, talkativeness, restlessness, jaw clenching, sweating, a pounding heart and anxiety increase. At very high levels, paranoia, hallucinations, overheating, chest pain, seizures, heart attack and stroke can occur.

### Why the same amount affects people differently

Why the same amount affects people differently: tolerance, body size, genetics, heart health, mental health history, sleep, heat, hydration, and other drugs (including antidepressants and alcohol) all change the effect. Street products vary hugely in strength and content.

## Overdose note for Amphetamine (Adderall, Dexedrine, Evekeo)

Amphetamine overdose causes chest pain, high blood pressure, overheating, seizures, stroke and psychosis.
