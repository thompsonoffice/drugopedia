# Carfentanil

> Educational harm-reduction content. Not medical advice, not a guide to use. Nothing here gives amounts, preparation, or sourcing. Any opioid can cause fatal overdose, and street drugs are frequently contaminated with fentanyl.

## At a glance

Synthetic opioid never approved for human use.

**Brand and street names:** Street names vary; often unlabeled.

## History and origins

Synthesized by Janssen in 1974 and used as a large-animal tranquilizer (Wildnil), about 100 times stronger than fentanyl. It appeared in illicit drugs in 2016 and again in 2024.

## Medical use, then and now

None in humans (veterinary tranquilizer only).

## How it entered recreational use

Sold as a 'research chemical' or found in counterfeit pills and powders.

## Legal status

US Schedule II (veterinary).

## What it feels like

### The character of its effects

Carfentanil produces opioid effects of warmth, sedation, and euphoria reported to be similar to other strong opioids. Reliable descriptions are scarce, and many people who took it did not know.

Its strength and duration are unpredictable in street products.

Tolerance changes everything here. Someone who has never used opioids feels far stronger effects, more nausea, and much higher overdose risk than someone who uses daily, and people who use every day often say they mainly feel 'normal' rather than high.

### Timeline

Times below are typical for the oral (swallowed) route and vary a lot between people. Other routes change the timing; see the route pages.

### Onset (15-45 min)

Fast onset, often seconds to minutes.

### Come-up (30-60 min)

Rapid build.

### Peak (1-2 h)

Heavy opioid peak.

### Plateau (2-4 h)

Short to moderate duration.

### Comedown (4-6 h)

Rapid comedown.

### After-effects (6-24 h)

Unknown.

### Next day

Dependence likely.

## Overdose note for Carfentanil

Very high overdose risk; carfentanil can kill in tiny amounts and may need multiple naloxone doses.

Read the shared page: [What an opioid overdose feels and looks like, and what to do](../safety/opioid-overdose.md). Also see [Xylazine (tranq)](../safety/xylazine.md). Also see [naloxone (Narcan)](../narcan/naloxone.md).
